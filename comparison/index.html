How the episode was rebuilt

The pipeline separates observation, evidence review, conversation composition, and media validation.

Sample the source1,200 clean frames at 2 fps cover the full episode.
Author candidatesQuestions, answers, evidence horizons, and event boundaries are proposed.
Require consensusEvery shipped candidate passes three visual reads; failures go to quarantine.
Compose and verifyAlternatives are timed, spoken, transcribed, rendered, and checked as complete artifacts.
Episode A is presented as a construction case study. The three rebuilt conversations are alternatives over one shared source episode, not simultaneous dialogue.
