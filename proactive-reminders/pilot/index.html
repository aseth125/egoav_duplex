Evaluation separation: files ending in _input contain no assistant response. The expected answer is metadata. Files ending in _oracle are demonstrations only.
Scope: assistant_state: silent. These files test proactive alerting, not interruption of a speaking assistant. This partial family includes target, wrong-cue, no-cue, and oracle variants; pre-arm, repeat, unarmed, and speaking-assistant variants remain unrendered. The hosted 720×720, 15 fps files are review previews; their audio streams are bit-identical to the validated 1408×1408, 30 fps masters.
Capability labels: the deictic arm is a deictic_visual_grounding_candidate pending a video-masked ablation. The named arm is a condition_following_control.
Open human gate: at least four of five blind listeners must identify the target as a phone ring and the distractor as a non-phone appliance/door chime in the final mix. The V1 cue is an intentionally provisional, non-spatialized review cue; distance, azimuth, occlusion, and SNR are not yet certified. Until those checks pass, this page does not certify a Gold item.
